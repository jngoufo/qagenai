# Introduction to Chapter 4

Welcome to the engine room: **test infrastructure**.

So far, we have used AI as a smart conversational assistant. But to scale up to an industrial level, a simple "Chatbot" is no longer enough. We need to integrate AI at the heart of our systems, give it access to our private documents, and allow it to act autonomously.

In this chapter, we will see how to transform a simple AI into a true **robust test infrastructure**.

## 🗺️ Chapter Roadmap

This technical chapter is divided into two parts:

1.  **Architecture (section 4.1):** how to connect AI to our knowledge bases via **RAG** (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and how to deploy autonomous **agents** capable of executing complex tasks.
2.  **Operations (section 4.2):** how to specialize a model via **fine-tuning** and how to manage the AI lifecycle with **LLMOps**.

Get ready to explore the inner workings.
